Baked Tortellini Casserole

Ingredients:

- 1 package (~540g) frozen cheese tortellini

- 500g ground beef

- 1 medium zucchini, sliced

- 1 small red onion, chopped

- 2 jars pasta sauce (maybe 1, let's see how it looks)

- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

- 1/2 cup cubed feta cheese

- 1.5 to 2 cups shredded mozzarella (or a blend with provolone/parmesan) (just pizza cheese)

- 2 tablespoons minced fresh basil, divided


Cooking instructions:

1. Prep: Preheat oven to 190°C (375°F). Lightly grease a large 9x13-inch (approx. 3-liter) baking dish.

2. Par-boil Tortellini: Cook frozen tortellini in boiling salted water for 2–3 minutes (it should still be firm in the center). Drain and set aside.

3. Brown & Combine: In a skillet, brown the beef, zucchini, and onion until meat is cooked through. Drain excess fat. Stir in pasta sauce, pepper, chopped tomatoes, and feta cheese.

4. Mix: Fold the par-cooked tortellini into the beef and sauce mixture until evenly coated.

5. Assemble & Bake: Transfer the mixture into the prepared baking dish. Top evenly with the shredded mozzarella and 1 tablespoon of fresh basil.

6. Bake: Bake uncovered for 20–25 minutes until the sauce is bubbling and the mozzarella is melted and lightly golden.

7. Finish: Let rest for 5 minutes before serving, then garnish with the remaining tablespoon of fresh basil.
