# Slow Cooked Tex-Mex Chili

## Ingredients

- 500g ground beef

- 400-500g black or kidney beans

- Taco seasoning 

- 1 can of crushed tomatoes

- 80-100g tomato puree

- 1 container hot sauce

- 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce

- 1 red or green bell pepper (chopped)

- 1/2 gold or white onion (chopped)

- 1 package of your favorite taco seasoning

- 10-15g (1-ish table spoon) of brown sugar

- Salt and pepper to taste

- A few shakes of or your favorite hot-chili sauce -- Tabasco, Cholula, etc.

## Cooking Instructions

1. Brown the meat in a skillet.

2. Throw the meat and literally everything else into a crock pot slow cooker.

3. Cook on low for at least 6 hours

## Serving Suggestion

- Tortilla chips

- Sour cream or creme fraiche

- Feta cheese, or shredded Monterey jack or cheddar cheese
