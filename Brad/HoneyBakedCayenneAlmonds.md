# Honey Coated Cayenne Almonds, or "*Beez Nutz*"

## Ingredients

For about 300 g nuts (almonds or pecans):

- 2 ½ cups (≈300 g) raw almonds or pecan halves
- ¼ cup (60 ml) honey
- 1 tbsp neutral oil (rapeseed/rapsolja or similar) – helps even coating
- 2–3 tbsp sugar (white or raw) for the crust
- ¾ tsp fine salt (or to taste)
- ½–1 tsp cayenne powder (adjust for heat)
- Optional: ½ tsp cinnamon or smoked paprika if you want extra warmth

## Cooking Instructions

### 1. Prep and mix coating

- Heat oven to 160–175 °C and line a tray with baking paper.
- In a bowl, mix sugar and salt (and any extra spices except cayenne). Set aside.
- Gently warm honey with the oil in a small saucepan or skillet until runny, then stir in cayenne. Do not boil.

### 2. Coat and roast

- Add nuts to the warm honey mixture and stir until everything is evenly coated.
- Spread in a single layer on the lined tray.
- Roast 10–15 minutes, stirring once or twice so they roast evenly and the honey doesn’t burn at the edges. Watch closely at the end.

### 3. Sugar crust and cooling

- Immediately when the nuts come out of the oven, toss them in the sugar–salt–spice mix until coated.
- Spread them out again on clean baking paper and separate clumps with two forks; let cool completely so the coating hardens and becomes crunchy.
