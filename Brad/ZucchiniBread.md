# Zucchini Bread

## Ingredients:

### Dry Ingredients:
 
 * 250 g (3.5dl) all-purpose wheat flour
  
 * 150 g (1.5dl) granulated sugar
   
 * 1 teaspoon baking powder 
   
 * 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
   
 * 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
   
 * 1/4 teaspoon salt
 
### Wet Ingredients:

* 2 large eggs

* 80 ml vegetable oil

* 1 teaspoon vanilla extract *or* 1/2 teaspoon vanilla powder

* 250 g shredded zucchini (about 1 medium-sized zucchini).

* 50 g chopped walnuts or pecans

* (Optional) 1 handful of raisins or dried cranberries

Equipment:
* Loaf pan (approx. 23 x 13 cm or 9 x 5 inches)

* Grater

* *Two* Mixing bowls

* Whisk

## Cooking Instructions:

*NOTE*: DO NOT combine the wet and dry ingredients until it's time (step 5, below). Otherwise the backing soda will activate prematurely and your zucchini bread will come out weird. 

1. Preheat and Prepare: Preheat your oven to 175°C (350°F). Grease and lightly flour your loaf pan, or line it with parchment paper.

2. Combine Dry Ingredients: In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, granulated sugar, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt. Make sure there are no lumps.

3. Combine Wet Ingredients: In *a separate* medium bowl, whisk together the eggs, vegetable oil, and vanilla extract until well combined.

4. Add Zucchini: Stir the grated zucchini into the wet ingredients.

5. Combine Wet and Dry: Pour the wet ingredient mixture into the dry ingredient mixture. Stir gently with a spoon or spatula until just combined. Be careful not to overmix, as this can make the bread tough.

6. Pour into Pan: Pour the batter evenly into your prepared loaf pan.

7. Bake: Bake for 45-60 minutes, or until a wooden skewer or toothpick inserted into the center of the loaf comes out clean. Baking time can vary depending on your oven and the moisture content of your zucchini.

8. Cool: Let the zucchini bread cool in the pan for about 10-15 minutes before carefully removing it and placing it on a wire rack to cool completely. This helps prevent it from breaking apart.

## Serving Suggestions:

- Sour creame or creme fraiche

- Cottage cheese

- Sauteed chaterelle mushrooms 

## Tips for Success:

* Keep the wet and dry ingredients separate until the last possible moment.

* Don't overmix: Overmixing develops gluten, which can lead to a tough bread. Mix just until the ingredients are combined.

* Grate your zucchini: Use the large holes on your grater.

* Storage: Zucchini bread keeps well at room temperature in an airtight container for 3-4 days. It can also be frozen for longer storage.
