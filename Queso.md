# Chili Con Queso

## Ingredients

- 30g butter

- 0.3 dl flour

- 0.25 dl cream

- 200g shredded gouda or food cheese

- 75g cream cheese

- fresh jalepeno

- kumin

- cayenne

- garlic powder

## Cooking Instructions
1. The Roux

- Cook butter & flour over med-low heat for about 1 minute, stir continuously

- whisk in milk until it thickens slightly

2. The Queso

- Adjust heat to low

- add cheese (shredded & cream) in small handfuls, stir until smooth

- stir in fine chopped fresh jalepeno, kumin, garlic powder, cayenne, pinch of salt

- thin with a little cream as needed
